# OpenAthan v0.4.0

Published stable release: [v0.4.0](https://github.com/OpenAthan-Project/openathan/releases/tag/v0.4.0).

The speaker's local device page now puts the next prayer and everyday controls
within easy reach on phones and desktops.

## What's new

- **Today** shows the next scheduled prayer, its device-local time and whether
  its Athan will play. Skipping an announcement keeps that prayer visible until
  its time passes, with a named Restore action.
- Volume and Skip sit directly below the next prayer. Stop remains available
  from Today and Settings while audio is playing.
- The timetable identifies enabled, disabled and skipped announcements, and
  marks Sunrise as having no Athan. Lost contact marks displayed information
  stale without claiming that the speaker stopped.
- **Settings** groups Athan preferences, prayer calculations, screen and lights,
  time format and updates. Everyday preferences save automatically, with nearby
  feedback. Location and calculation changes require timetable preview and
  confirmation.
- First-run setup guides you through location, calculation conventions and
  timetable review. Manual setup works locally; the location helper is optional.
- Saved 12/24-hour formatting applies to the device page and optional display.
  Screen brightness is adjustable and defaults to 50% when no preference is saved.
  Existing saved preferences are retained.

The device page uses local, compressed assets. The approved Athan recordings,
partition layout and Home Assistant independence are unchanged.

## Updating your speaker

Speakers running v0.2.1 or v0.3.0 can check for
updates on their device page and choose **Install update**. Compatible application
updates preserve settings, credentials, prayer history and stored audio.

**Fresh USB installation erases saved settings, credentials and prayer history.**
Existing speakers should follow the [preserving update guidance](../development/firmware-upgrades.md).

The reference hardware remains AtomS3R C126 with GC9107 + Voice Pyramid A167.
ST7735 display revisions remain unqualified.

See the [preparation and acceptance record](../development/v0.4.0-preparation-2026-10-07.md)
for measured builds and the current physical-validation status.
