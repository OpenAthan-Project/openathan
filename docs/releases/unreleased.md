# Unreleased

## One hold to skip or restore Athan

Hold the front or BOOT button for about three seconds, then release to skip the
next Athan. Repeat the same hold to restore it. This works on all three supported
boards; restoring no longer needs a seven-second hold. Short-press Stop and phone
controls keep their existing behavior.

See the [validation record](../development/skip-toggle-validation-2026-11-13.md)
for automated checks, firmware measurements and pending physical validation.

Default-on automatic updates are documented in [v0.11.0](v0.11.0.md).
