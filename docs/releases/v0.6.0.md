# OpenAthan v0.6.0

**Release candidate:** publication and stable/latest selection are pending.
The public installer continues to serve the selected published release.

## What's included

- Listen to Quran from the device's local phone page. Search reciters, choose an
  available edition and surah, and use Play or Stop. Quran streaming needs Wi-Fi
  and access to MP3Quran; scheduled Athan still uses the stored recordings.
- Scheduled Athan takes priority over Quran. Stop cancels pending or active Quran
  playback, and an interrupted stream does not resume automatically.
- Waveshare's prayer ring stays visible during Quran, Athan, setup, clock-waiting
  and errors. It follows the next calculated prayer; unavailable trustworthy
  timing shows gray. Playback messages and stop guidance stay in the center.
- Atom/Voice Pyramid volume uses a calibrated range and checked output controls.
  Updating converts saved levels to preserve approximately the previous loudness.
  Audio cleanup also handles a delayed Stop without latching the reproduced DMA
  completion fault.
- Invalid Quran input that ends without decoded audio reports a playback error
  rather than appearing to finish normally.

## Install and update

Supported hardware remains AtomS3R C126 + Voice Pyramid A167 and Waveshare
ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.85C-BOX V2. Waveshare V1 is not supported.

After publication, use the signed Wi-Fi updater on the existing device's settings
page to preserve saved data. Fresh installation through
[the public installer](https://openathan.com/install/) erases existing firmware,
Wi-Fi credentials, settings and prayer history. Preserving USB-update availability
continues to follow the installer's reviewed release policy.

Start with a low volume and increase gradually. The Atom's calibrated maximum
has greater output range; its new maximum has not been qualified for distortion.

## Validation and limits

Attended Waveshare development checks confirmed the prayer ring during Quran
and real scheduled Maghrib playback and afterward, with clear audio. Controlled
Quran failure/recovery and Athan priority have separate source-bound evidence.
These development results do not establish acceptance of every exact release
image, board, recording or network condition.

Touch input, microphones and SD storage remain unsupported. RTC backup-battery
operation, unplugged time retention and long-term drift remain unqualified.
Clean-production runtime memory and longer repeated streaming remain unmeasured.
See the [release preparation and evidence limits](../development/v0.6.0-preparation-2026-10-14.md)
and [Quran hardware validation](../development/quran-hardware-validation-2026-10-14.md).

Stored Athan recordings retain their existing approval and attribution in
[AUDIO-LICENSES.md](../../AUDIO-LICENSES.md). Quran recordings are streamed from
the provider and are not added to the release bundle.
