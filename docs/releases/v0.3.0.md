# OpenAthan v0.3.0

A dim, always-on display now shows your local clock, next prayer and Athan playback status.

## What’s new

- See the next prayer and time, whether it will be skipped, and when Athan is playing. The screen also shows disabled prayers and when setup or time synchronization is needed.
- Place the Pyramid’s ports and power cord at the back. Text is upright when viewed from the opposite side, with brightness set to 10% by default.
- If Wi-Fi disconnects, an **Offline** footer appears. Scheduled Athan continues while the clock remains valid.

The Athan recordings and existing controls are unchanged. Display problems do not stop scheduling, audio or device controls.

## Updating your speaker

For speakers running **v0.2.1**, open the speaker’s device page, check for updates and choose **Install update**. Compatible updates keep your settings, Wi-Fi credentials, prayer history and stored audio.

**Fresh USB installation erases saved settings, credentials and prayer history.** For an existing speaker, follow the [preserving update guidance](https://github.com/OpenAthan-Project/openathan/blob/1915fe0a161640518f25d967ea3e58d5bc65b93d/docs/development/firmware-upgrades.md).

See the [dated release evidence](../development/release-validation-2026-10-04.md) for the complete measurements and delivery results.

## Supported hardware

Validated on the **M5Stack AtomS3R C126 with a GC9107 display and Voice Pyramid A167**. Newer **ST7735 display revisions have not been validated**.
