# OpenAthan v0.10.0

Release candidate. See the
[preparation record](../development/v0.10.0-preparation-2026-11-10.md)
for qualification results and pending checks.

## Find your speakers

Open `http://openathan.local/` from a browser on your home Wi-Fi to see your
OpenAthan speakers. Choose a speaker to open its settings and sign in with its
device password.

At least one speaker needs this firmware to host the finder. Older speakers
that advertise their settings page can appear by their short device ID.
Speakers running this firmware can take over hosting the finder when its host
goes offline. Your Wi-Fi needs to allow devices to reach each other.

## Name each speaker

Save a name such as Kitchen or Bedroom in Setup or Settings. The name appears
on that speaker's page and in the finder. A short device ID distinguishes
speakers with the same name. Clearing the saved name restores the OpenAthan
label.

Saved names are visible to people who can reach the speakers on your local
network, before they sign in.

## Listen to Quran

The Quran page puts the current recording, Stop and speaker volume together.
Changing the next selection keeps the playing recording's label unchanged.
On phones, playback controls stay visible while browsing when space allows.

Reciters load when you first open the page. Recording editions and surahs appear
as their data becomes available. Failed catalog requests offer Retry without
restarting playback. Quran streaming still needs internet access; scheduled
Athan takes priority and does not resume the interrupted Quran recording.

## Other changes

The AUDIO-Board guide links an optional
[3D-printable lid](../../hardware/reference-builds/waveshare-audio-board/README.md).
Contributor checks now skip firmware builds for documentation-only pull requests
and run Chromium and WebKit separately. Main pushes retain the complete checks.

## Update

Supported hardware remains AtomS3R C126 with Voice Pyramid A167, Waveshare Box V2
and Waveshare AUDIO-Board. Choose the matching hardware profile.

For an existing speaker, use the signed application update through its settings
page or the preserving USB updater. These updates retain credentials, settings,
prayer history and recordings. Publishing a release does not install it on speakers.

A fresh installation through the [USB installer](https://openathan.com/install/)
erases existing firmware, credentials, settings and prayer history. Use USB
recovery for Wi-Fi or password changes. If the finder does not open, use the
speaker's individual local hostname or current IP address.

## Testing and limitations

All three production profiles passed build, capacity, signature and installer
validation at the source recorded in the preparation report. Representative
Box V2 checks covered preserving updates, names, finder access and Quran
Play, Stop and replay. Owner-reported iPhone Safari and Android Chrome checks
passed on firmware with matching committed runtime and UI sources.

Exact final-image installation and the offline and two-speaker takeover checks
remain pending. Earlier playback and runtime-memory results retain their source
and workload limits. Review the [qualification record](../development/v0.10.0-preparation-2026-11-10.md)
before installing this candidate.

The approved normal, Fajr and dua recordings are unchanged. See
[recording attribution and permission](../../AUDIO-LICENSES.md).
