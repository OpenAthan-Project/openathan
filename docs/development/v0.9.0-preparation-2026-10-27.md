# v0.9.0 preparation — October 27, 2026

This proposed release adds the screenless Waveshare ESP32-S3-AUDIO-Board to the
existing Atom and Box V2 release. The support implementation merged as
`61061e2a8d7563a0685895cf4a8552a71e3eeb68`; the website implementation merged as
`e1f2a4c030dbc9d70e3d829444dc88b4ada98d1d`. This preparation changes the version
and release documentation, without changing application behavior or trust.

The [proposed release notes](../releases/v0.9.0.md) describe setup and controls.
The [release guide](releases.md) documents the three-board package. It contains
twenty-one assets with approved dua, or nineteen without it. All three hardware
profiles share one audio image and retain the existing protocol versions,
two 2 MiB application slots, separate 3.5 MiB audio partition and 1,572,864-byte
application limit. Older single-board and two-board bundles remain supported.

## Reused hardware evidence and remaining gates

The [October 21 hardware report](waveshare-audio-board-hardware-2026-10-21.md)
records initial installation, phone setup, controls, playback, lights and powered
RTC restoration. Its October 22 follow-up records automatic Fajr and dua followed
by a quiet restart. The [October 26 update report](waveshare-audio-board-upgrades-2026-10-26.md)
adds signed HTTP/USB updates, corruption/transport/cancellation cases, power cuts,
startup rollback, scheduled retained recordings and a full one-hour private-image
runtime trace. Fresh full-flash readback verified preservation and restoration;
the owner confirmed the regular page, password, timetable and saved volume.
These accepted checks retain their exact source, image and one-unit limits.

The isolated Wi-Fi wizard loop and one esptool RTS restart remain unresolved.
Exact production release signing/feed/redirect qualification and physical
public-installer fresh-install, preserving update, retry and recovery remain
separate checks. The unknown PCB revision and untested battery, microphone,
storage/display expansion, maximum-output and overnight behavior are not new
passes. Runtime results from a private qualification image do not establish
exact production-image memory headroom.

After review and merge, require successful exact-main CI, rebuild that exact
commit and revalidate the signed three-board bundle before creating a version
tag or uploading an unpublished GitHub draft. Coordinate concrete remaining
hardware checks with the owner and preserve current records and recovery first.
Publication as stable/latest and automatic website adoption require explicit
approval after the concrete candidate and its qualification are reviewed.
